## Cast in this episode

- **Kuman** — small black ink silhouette child, round head, glowing white eyes, always seated
- **Khun Anan** — white stick figure man, buttoned shirt lines, wristwatch as a small circle
- **Phra Sombat** — white stick figure monk, robe drawn as a single draped white outline over one shoulder, shaved head

> **Gold in monochrome:** the gold leaf is rendered as small bright white flecks and
> hard highlights on the black silhouette — never as tone or colour. Kuman is the
> only entity in the series that carries any highlight at all.

> **Kuman is never drawn standing or walking.** He is always seated, and he changes
> position only between shots, never within one. That restraint is the whole scare.
